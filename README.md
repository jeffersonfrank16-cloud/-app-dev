# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## Overview
**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi horror drama set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana. It blends supernatural mystery, government conspiracy, and heartfelt coming-of-age storytelling.

## Main Characters
- **Eleven** – A girl with telekinetic powers who escapes from a secret laboratory
- **Mike Wheeler** – A loyal friend who leads the search for their missing companion
- **Joyce Byers** – A determined mother who will do anything to find her son
- **Jim Hopper** – The town’s chief of police, uncovering dark secrets

## Why I Love It
> "It perfectly balances nostalgia, suspense, and genuine heart — every season leaves you wanting more."

-  1980s pop culture references and retro aesthetic
-  Gripping storylines that keep you guessing
-  Strong themes of friendship and courage
-  Stellar performances and memorable soundtrack
